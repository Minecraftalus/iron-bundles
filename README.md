# Iron Bundles

Iron Bundles adds 6 new tiers of bundles, with increasing sizes
- Copper - 1.5 stacks of regular items
- Iron - 2 stacks of regular items
- Gold - 4 stacks of regular items
- Emerald - 6 stacks of regular items
- Diamond - 10 stacks of regular items
- Netherite - 16 stacks of regular items

Each tier is crafted using the corresponding upgrade item.
The first upgrade uses a + shape of string surrounded by Copper Ingots.
For every subsequent upgrade, the string is replaced by the previous tier's upgrade.

The Netherite upgrade is crafted in a smithing table, as any other netherite item.
